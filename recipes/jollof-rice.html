Jollof Rice Recipe

Description

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, and a variety of spices. It's a flavorful and colorful dish that is often served at parties and special occasions.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 can (400g) diced tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 2 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: cooked chicken, shrimp, or vegetables for added protein

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice in cold water until the water runs clear. Set aside.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and red bell pepper, and sauté until softened.
  3. Add the diced tomatoes and tomato paste to the pot. Cook for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Add the thyme, curry powder, paprika, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine.
  5. Add the rinsed rice to the pot and stir to coat the rice with the tomato mixture.
  6. Pour in the chicken or vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for about 20-25 minutes, or until the rice is cooked and the liquid is absorbed.
  7. If desired, add cooked chicken, shrimp, or vegetables during the last 5 minutes of cooking.
  8. Fluff the rice with a fork and serve hot. Enjoy your delicious Jollof rice!
