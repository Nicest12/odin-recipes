Noodles Recipe

Description

Noodles are a simple and delicious meal that can be prepared quickly!

Ingredients:

  • 200g noodles
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrots, bell peppers, broccoli)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Boil water in a pot.
  2. Add the noodles and cook according to the package instructions.
  3. Drain the noodles when they are cooked.
  4. Add the noodles back to the pot and mix in the seasoning.
  5. Serve hot and enjoy.
